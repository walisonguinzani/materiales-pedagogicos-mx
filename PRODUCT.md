# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS plus Vercel serverless functions (`api/`), same architecture as the owner's `caligrafia-mexico` site. Delegated: chosen to reuse the working Zenith Payments (SPEI) checkout already built there.

## Users

Christian adults in Mexico (mostly women: Sunday-school and children's-ministry teachers, catechists, small-group leaders, Christian-school teachers, mothers) arriving on their phone from Meta ads. Spanish (Mexico), informal "tú".

## Product Purpose

Sell "Materiales pedagógicos para la educación cristiana": the Kit Semillas de Esperanza (printable evangelization fold-out cards) with two bonuses. Success = visitor pays by SPEI. After the signed Zenith webhook confirms payment, the buyer gets the link to the WhatsApp Grupo VIP; the PDFs are never delivered by the site or stored in the repo.

## Positioning

Ready-to-print material that turns sharing the Gospel into a small gift: each card folds into an illustration (sunflower, heart, flowers, sun, Bible, Jesus, dove, butterfly) and opens to a message and an NTV verse. Usable in Sunday school, Christian schools, values class, cells, visits and at home.

## Operating Context

Print at home or at a papelería on letter/A4 (opalina or thin cardstock), at 100 %; cut, fold, hand out. Delivery and community happen in the WhatsApp Grupo VIP (weekly news and devotionals).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Kit Semillas de Esperanza: 8 designs × 4 messages = 32 biblical messages, assembly instructions.
- Bonus 1: Tarjetas de Oración, 12 cards (one per month).
- Bonus 2: Guía "Cómo compartir sin incomodar".
- One single plan, one-time payment of $150 MXN. SPEI only, via Zenith checkout.
- No refund guarantee (owner's decision): never show a guarantee.
- Personal, family, ministry and evangelistic use; resale or sharing the digital files is not permitted (stated in the kit).
- Bible text: Nueva Traducción Viviente (NTV), © Tyndale House Foundation; credit line required where verses appear.
- Footer must carry Términos y Condiciones, Aviso de Privacidad and a Meta/Facebook non-affiliation disclaimer.
- Grupo VIP link is shown only after confirmed payment.

## Brand Commitments

Brand name: "Educación de la Esperanza". Contact: goatgateag@gmail.com. Visual identity binding: follow the kit's own identity (warm cream paper, olive/sage green, soft gold, serif titles with wide-tracked small caps, watercolor sticker illustrations, sprout icon). Presenter photo of a smiling teacher in a classroom (assets).

## Evidence on Hand

Covers of the kit, the prayer cards and the guide (cover previews only, no inner pages beyond the cover). Teacher photo. No testimonials, no sales numbers: do not fabricate any. No earnings claims, no resale pitch.

## Product Principles

- Show the real cover and the fold-out idea; the object sells itself.
- One price, one action, no confusion.
- Warm, reverent, respectful: no hype, no fake scarcity, no invented "before" prices.
